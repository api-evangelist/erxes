# Erxes Plans

Erxes offers a self-hosted open-source edition (AGPLv3) and a cloud-hosted SaaS model with tiered plans. Pricing is structured per product module (e.g., Frontline, Sales, Operations). The following reflects the Frontline product tier as of June 2026.

- **Cost:** $0/month

- 5,000 contacts
- 5 pop-ups & forms
- 1 knowledge base
- 1 portal
- 3 automations
- 5 segments
- 5 ticket management slots
- 5 task management slots
- 5 sales pipeline slots
- 5,000 emails/month

## Self-Hosted (Community Edition)

- **Cost:** Free (AGPLv3 license)
- Full feature access via self-hosting
